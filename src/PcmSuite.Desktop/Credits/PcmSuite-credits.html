PcmSuite

PcmSuite is a prototype that puts flashing, logging and tuning in one window. It is built on the work of the people and projects listed below, and on PCM Hammer's own credits further down this page.

PCM Hammer - The Flash and Logger tabs are PCM Hammer's own screens, used unmodified from its source tree (github.com/PcmHammer/PcmHammer, GPLv3 except the PCM-side kernel). Reading, writing, kernels, logging, protocols, checksum validation, and the Avalonia user interface all come from it. The kernel files are not part of PcmSuite: they come from PCM Hammer's official releases, under its licence.

Universal Patcher - By Joukoy and contributors (universalpatcher.net, GPLv3). The Tuner's XDF reader is written to cope with XDF files that Universal Patcher exports, and the table-reference idea in equations follows its convention. No Universal Patcher source code is included.

TunerPro - The XDF definition format that the Tuner reads (tunerpro.net), and the community of people who write and share XDF files.

Libraries - Avalonia, FluentAvalonia, CommunityToolkit.Mvvm and DynamicExpresso.
